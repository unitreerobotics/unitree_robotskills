# Capture Removal: Same-Side Round Tray of the Executing Hand

English | [中文](capture_tray.zh.md)

The round trays on both sides of the board serve as the storage target for captured pieces: when the physical left hand performs the removal, the piece goes into the left tray; when the physical right hand does, it goes into the right tray. The side is judged by the robot's own left and right, not by camera stream names or the logical `right_arm` field. The target of our own piece is still the original capture target intersection.

During planning, locate the same-side tray in this round's head image and choose a landing point within the usable area inside the tray, avoiding the tray rim and pieces already stored there. XY is estimated from this round's image and the tool pose. The release height for both left- and right-hand trays is uniformly fixed at robot base coordinate Z=0.159m; do not reuse an old board target or an arbitrary tabletop point. The landing point is not required to be at the tray centre. If there is no usable area inside the tray, do not switch on your own to the other side or outside the tray.

Fill in `capture.offboard_target`:

- `kind: "round_tray"`;
- `physical_side: "left"` or `"right"`, matching this round's physical executing hand;
- `xy_m`, fixed `z_m: 0.159`, `rpy_rad` and this round's image `evidence`;
- `capture.offboard_surface_review` records the usable area inside the tray, the tray rim/existing pieces, support height, tool clearance and retreat path.

The template pre-fills the tray `z_m=0.159`; XY is filled in from this round's image; both hands use this release height. The planner rejects removal targets that are not a tray, whose side does not match, or whose release Z is not 0.159m, and records the tray pose and its basis in `plan_summary.capture_target`. This validation checks the semantics and side of what is filled in; it does not replace image-based localisation or the complete service preflight.

For a capture, two transactions are prepared jointly in advance and the sequence is executed only once: first move the captured piece into the same-side tray; once the release opening is complete, immediately retreat vertically along the preflighted path and pass through the clearance staging point to the high position above our own piece's source for a re-capture of images. staging is only a motion waypoint: it does not wait for manual operation, another image capture or a service request; the second segment keeps the gripper open and does not open it again. Return to init only after our own piece is placed on the target intersection. Stop the hand-over if the opening is not complete or motion protection refuses. This rule is the same for the left and right hands and for any captured piece; the entire sequence is executed by the same physical hand.

Before releasing into the tray, first descend to the planned Z=0.159m and re-capture, relying mainly on the executing hand's wrist image with the head image as an aid. Release as soon as the piece is predicted to land entirely within the usable area of the same-side tray while avoiding the tray rim and existing pieces; precise centring is not required, and the board intersection criterion does not apply. Outside the tray, pressing on the rim, interfering with existing pieces, a landing point that truly cannot be judged, or a collision risk are handled with corrections as needed. The piece covering the target point inside the tray does not by itself count as unclear. Use a justified non-zero low-position XY correction and keep the actual Z/RPY; there is no single or cumulative distance limit and no motion check of the correction segment. At most 3 attempts: re-capture after the first two; after the third, open the gripper in place directly without an additional re-capture or approval condition. Actual hardware or control faults are still handled by the original procedure.

This package's planner generates `alignment_criterion=actor_wrist_predicted_landing_inside_same_side_tray` for the removal transaction, and the preflight must return the same criterion; the tray criterion in the plan and in the preflight receipt must match. Actual execution uses only the planner, service and new token from this deployment's package root. Updates on disk are not loaded automatically into existing processes; for versions and the implementation boundary of the correction policy see [runtime](runtime.md). On a new device, first check the compatibility of the trays and the fixed height according to [first setup](first_game_setup.md).
