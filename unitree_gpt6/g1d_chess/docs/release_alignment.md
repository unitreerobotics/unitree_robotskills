# Expected Landing Point and Target Intersection Judgment Before Release

English | [中文](release_alignment.zh.md)

After descending to release height and before opening the gripper, recapture and judge the piece's expected landing point from the physical acting hand's wrist camera, with the head image helping to check the target square name and neighbors. The expected landing point should be near the corresponding intersection; slight eccentricity is allowed and perfectly precise alignment is not required. Also check that the whole piece drops without overlapping neighbors, and that opening the gripper and retracting vertically have no obvious interference. For neighbor relations see [Neighbor Clearance and Accurate Placement](neighbor_clearance_and_landing.md).

| Expected landing point supported by the acting-hand wrist image | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Between two adjacent intersections | `adjust`, correct toward the target intersection |
| In the middle of a square | `adjust`, correct toward the target intersection |
| Clearly off the corresponding target intersection, including close to a wrong intersection | `adjust`, correct toward the corresponding target intersection |
| Near the corresponding target intersection, only slightly eccentric and safely releasable | `success`, open the gripper in place |
| The piece covers the target intersection, but the landing point can be judged from visible grid lines and the piece center | Not a case of being unable to see; decide by whether the expected landing point is close to the target intersection, do not correct because of occlusion itself |
| The expected landing point genuinely cannot be judged, or there is a collision risk | Handle as needing correction, `adjust`; state the uncertainty or risk and the basis for the correction |

What is judged is the piece's expected landing point after the gripper opens, which must take into account gripping eccentricity, parallax between the piece's top face and the board surface, and the relation between the piece and the target grid lines. The gripper housing, shadows, a piece edge covering the intersection, and a small end-effector FK error cannot alone prove the landing point is aligned. Do not hard-set millimeter/pixel thresholds when uncalibrated. `reason` and `evidence` state this step's acting-hand wrist image path, the relation between the expected landing point and the target intersection, and the correction direction.

The piece covering the target intersection by itself is not a case of being unable to see; the intersection is not required to be fully exposed. Infer the expected landing point from extended visible grid lines, nearby intersections, the piece center and parallax; covering the intersection by itself also cannot prove alignment. Only when this information is still insufficient to judge the landing point is it genuinely unclear.

The submission format has seven fields, and `obvious_large_offset` distinguishes whether correction is needed: for the obvious deviations needing correction above, a genuinely undeterminable expected landing point or a collision risk, it is true, with `release_alignment_verified=false`; for slight eccentricity near the intersection that is releasable, it is false. `release_alignment_verified=true` means it has been checked that the expected landing point is near the corresponding target intersection and can be safely released, not that it is precisely centered. A classification needing correction is not a license for arbitrary displacement: submitting adjust still requires identifying the target and giving a justified non-zero XY; target identification or correction displacement must not be fabricated. XY correction has no per-step limit or cumulative distance limit relative to the originally planned target, no correction-segment motion check, and at most 3 times. Image semantic judgment is done by the main session; for differences between the operating specification and the runtime implementation see [runtime](runtime.md#implementation-verification-boundary).

Plans generated by this package's planner and the service pre-check must match `adjustment_policy=obvious_off_target_intersection` and `alignment_criterion=actor_wrist_predicted_landing_near_target_intersection`. The policy fields in the plan and the pre-check receipt must be consistent. Capture removal uses `alignment_criterion=actor_wrist_predicted_landing_inside_same_side_tray`: the left hand places into the tray on the left of the board, the right hand into the tray on the right, and the tray release height for both hands is Z=0.159m. Release once the piece is expected to land entirely within the usable area inside the tray, avoiding the tray rim and already placed pieces; precise alignment to the tray center is not required. Outside the tray, pressing on the tray rim, interfering with existing pieces, or a genuinely undeterminable landing point requires correction. The piece covering the target point inside the tray by itself does not count as unclear. For details see [Tray Capture Rules](capture_tray.md); the intersection rules above are for placing our own pieces.

The action order is target high position, descend, recapture while holding, decide, open the gripper. Corrections are relative to this step's actual low-position FK; keep the actual Z/RPY and adjust XY directly without lifting; at most 3 times: recapture after the first two, and after the third correction completes release directly by opening the gripper in place, with no further recapture or visual approval; no correction-segment motion check is performed, and actual hardware or control faults are still handled by the original flow. For the complete flow see [Operation Manual 0.5.2](operation_manual.md) and [round_plan step 4](../prompts/round_plan.md).
